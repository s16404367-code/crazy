Race tips

  • Brake at the white marker boards for the Hairpin, Turn 1 and the Chicane.
  • Soft tyres are quickest but wear fast — in a 10-lap race, plan a stop.
  • Following closely gives a slipstream tow; open the AERO on both straights.
  • Heavy damage costs grip & top speed — press P and let the autopilot pit for repairs.
